Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Here is everything you need to know about Lumiqo.

Is Lumiqo free to download and use?
Yes! Lumiqo is completely free to download from the Google Play Store. Core reading features and standard PDF utility tools are free to use.
Are my PDF documents uploaded to external servers?
No, never! Lumiqo processes all PDF modifications, conversions, and rendering locally on your Android device. Your private documents never leave your phone.
Which PDF editing tools are included in Lumiqo?
Lumiqo includes 15+ built-in tools: Merge, Split, Compress, Delete Pages, Extract Pages, Reorder Pages, PDF to Image, Image to PDF, PDF to Word/Excel/PPT, Web to PDF, Protect PDF, Unlock PDF, Watermark, and Digital Signature.
What advantages does Lumiqo Pro offer?
Lumiqo Pro unlocks unlimited batch processing, advanced OCR text recognition, high-resolution conversions, password protection features, cloud sync backup, and completely removes all advertisements.
