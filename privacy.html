Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our highest priority. Lumiqo is built to be a local-first PDF utility where your documents remain 100% your own.

Effective Date: June 21, 2026  •  Last Updated: October 19, 2026

Lumiqo Labs ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Lumiqo PDF Reader mobile application for Android. This Privacy Policy informs you of our practices regarding the handling, storage, and protection of your data.

Strict Privacy Commitment: Lumiqo is a local utility. We do NOT collect, upload, transmit, or share the content of your PDF files. All processing (reading, favoriting, renaming, deleting, merging, splitting, compressing, and converting) occurs strictly on your device.

1. Information We Handle

Lumiqo is designed to function seamlessly without requiring personal user account creation.

A. User-Generated Content (PDFs)

The app reads and processes your PDF documents locally. These files never leave your device memory. We do not have access to your private files, reading materials, or documents.

B. Locally Stored Data

To deliver a personalized experience, the following data is stored exclusively on your device using Jetpack Room Database and DataStore preferences:

  • Reading Progress & Memory (Last read page, total minutes read per day, and page bookmarks)
  • App Preferences (Light, Dark, or Sepia theme selections, layout settings)
  • Library Metadata (Recent file history and Favorites list)

2. Device Permissions

To provide core PDF document viewing and editing functionality, Lumiqo requests standard Android system permissions:

  • Storage Access (Android 12 and below): Standard external storage read/write permissions to scan storage, index your local PDF library, and support file renaming and safe deletion.
  • Scoped Storage & File Picker (Android 13+): No broad storage permissions required at runtime. Uses system MediaStore indexing and secure Storage Access Framework (SAF) pickers so your documents remain sandboxed.
  • Internet & Notifications: Required to deliver system announcements, updates, and optional daily reading goal reminders via Firebase Cloud Messaging.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted industry providers solely for essential app distribution, messaging, and maintenance:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Delivers broadcast push notifications and system updates. FCM may use an anonymous "Instance ID" token to deliver messages.
  • Google Play Services: Used for secure app distribution, update checks, and crash analytics.

4. Monetization & Payments

In-app upgrades for Lumiqo Pro are processed securely through Razorpay and Google Play Billing. We do not store, process, or see your credit card details or financial identifiers on our servers.

5. Children's Privacy

Lumiqo is a general productivity application and does not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
